Make it update properly

iPhones check subscribed calendars once a week by default, so timing changes will not reach you. Go to Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Fetch New Data → Refresh Calendars, and choose the shortest option.

On an iPhone but you use the Google Calendar app? The Google Calendar app cannot add a calendar on a phone, on any platform. Use the Add to Google Calendar button above once from a computer, and it will then appear in the app on your phone. If you would rather not use a computer, use the iPhone Calendar button instead and the miqaats will show in the Calendar app that came with your phone.

About the timings. Dates come from the Misri calendar. Times follow the jamaat's own announcements and firm up in the week before each program, so anything further out is a placeholder. Reconcile against the printed taqweem.

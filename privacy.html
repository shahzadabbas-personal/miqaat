Miqaat Sweep privacy policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Miqaat Sweep is a personal tool that keeps the Anjuman-e-Burhani New Jersey miqaat calendar up to date. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

What it reads. Miqaat announcement emails from the jamaat, using read-only Gmail access.

What it keeps. Only the miqaat name, date, start time and venue. Email bodies are held in memory while being read and then discarded. They are never saved, logged or shared.

What it changes. Event times on one Google Calendar, the public miqaat calendar. It touches no other calendar.

Who else sees your data. Announcement text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to extract the miqaat details, under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not permit training on it. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
